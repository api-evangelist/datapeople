The world of Talent Acquisition (TA) is in a fascinating, albeit challenging, paradox. Organizations are demanding unprecedented levels of productivity and impact from their TA teams, often with fewer resources. With global headcount down by an average of 25% compared to two or three years ago, recruiters are now responsible for 30-60% more requisitions.
