Washington D.C. is taking a significant step towards greater wage equity with its Pay Transparency-focused Wage Transparency Omnibus Amendment Act of 2023. As of June 2024, this law stipulates that employers will include minimum and maximum projected salary or hourly pay in all job listings and position descriptions advertised.
